Chapter 4

Boss Rush

Jinx leads the Squad to the Glitch King's tower, where beating the boss would bring back everything he deleted. Ninety-nine floors later, only Player 1 can open the final door. Zuggi does what he always does, and the King was counting on it.

Meet the players

  • Zuggi

    Player 1. The only one who can open the door.

  • Kai Vega

    Already planning pizza for after.

  • Rei Kurogane

    Keeping two secrets. Watching the cat.

  • Byte

    Builds disguises. Carries a voice.

  • Jinx

    Has a job to do for the King.

The Glitch King's tower — The same night

The King doesn't want Zuggi deleted. He wants something only Zuggi can do.

An hour later the cat is back, and it found exactly what the Squad needs.

A rule every gamer knows: beat the boss, and everything he took comes back. The power. The deleted people. All of it.

Rei hasn't told anyone what she read on that wall. Instead she's going to do the next best thing: keep the cat where she can see it.

The tower gates

The front door is guarded. Byte has a plan. It involves boxes.

Stealth mode lasted eleven seconds. Combat mode lasts ninety-nine floors.

One elevator ride to the top. Possibly the last quiet minute they'll have.

Floor 100 — The final door

Only Player 1 can open the King's door. And in the Grid, a team's shields come from Player 1. If he quits, everyone loses their protection.

Zuggi knows this. He also knows what happened the last time he faced a final boss.

He does what he always does.

The throne room

The King never needed Zuggi to open the door. He needed Zuggi to run.

Rei has been carrying her brother's warning since Chapter 2. It just came out.

Jinx has deleted things for the King before. Never like this. Never people it had started to like.

Meanwhile — Far below

Zuggi runs until the Grid runs out. Then it spits him back into the real world.

The throne room

Back in the cage, Byte does it again. This time it isn't one word.

Next — Chapter 5: Continue? Zuggi is alone in a city going grey. And someone he didn't expect comes to find him.
