Chapter 2

Tutorial Level

Inside the Grid, every player gets a power: Zuggi a combo-dash, Kai a grind-anything board, Rei a code-cutting blade, Byte the ground itself. They bicker, Kai's plan nearly gets them deleted, and they only make it out as a team. Then Rei finds a message carved by her missing brother.

Meet the players

    Power: combo-dash. Still has never finished a game.

  • Kai Vega

    Power: grind any surface. Plans still terrible.

  • Rei Kurogane

    Power: cut through code. Looking for her brother.

  • Byte

  • Jinx

The Grid — Tutorial Level

Nobody falls into the Grid. You log in, you log out. Tonight four people fell in, and there is no log-out button.

In the Grid, real-world rules are gone. Every player gets a power, and it's always tied to who they are.

Zuggi, who never sits still, gets a combo-dash. Kai, who never brakes, can grind any surface. Rei, who never misses, can cut through code itself.

And Byte, built to find a way when there isn't one, can hack the ground under their feet.

Every tutorial ends with a test. This one is simple: reach the exit before the timer runs out.

Four strangers. One exit. Sixty seconds. What could go wrong?

Fifteen seconds wasted arguing about who's in charge. Naturally, Kai has an idea.

Kai's plan, in full: grind the shortcut. Nobody told him the shortcut was a firewall.

When the Grid finds an error, it deletes it. And right now, the error is them.

The last platform

They left Jinx in the alley in Nexa City. Jinx, apparently, found another way in.

No time for the cat. The exit is one jump away, and the floor behind them is gone. This is the part where Zuggi always quits.

Kai's second plan is better, because it isn't his. It's everyone's: Byte builds, Kai rides, Rei cuts, Zuggi carries.

One gap left, too far for anyone to jump alone. Lucky for them, Zuggi's dash works better with passengers.

The exit

They made it, together. And for the first time in his life, Zuggi didn't quit.

Past the exit, while the boys celebrate, Rei finds a wall that shouldn't be there.

REN_K. Her brother's username. He was here. And he left a warning.
