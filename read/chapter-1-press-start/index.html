Chapter 1

Press Start

Zuggi rage-quits the Grid Cup final, then watches a billboard get deleted pixel by pixel. A cat steals his headphones, a glitch-beast attacks, Kai skates in, Rei cuts it in half, and Byte finds Player 1. Then the alley falls into the Grid.

Meet the players

  • Jinx

    A black cat nobody owns. Steals things. Has plans.

  • Rei Kurogane

  • Byte

Nexa City — Tonight

Every screen in Nexa City runs on the same game network: the Grid. People play it to work, to shop, to compete — and to forget.

Tonight is the Grid Cup Final. The whole city is watching one player.

His name is Zuggi. Best player in the city... on paper.

His opponent is no one on the leaderboard. A crowned figure made of static. And Zuggi's health is down to 1%.

Zuggi has a secret: he has never finished a single game. When the final boss shows up, he bails.

So he does what he always does.

Outside Pixel Palace

Zuggi walks out into the rain, hood up, trying not to hear the crowd.

Billboards don't glitch in Nexa City. Not like that. Something just deleted it — in the real world.

And someone has been watching him the whole time.

The cat's name is Jinx. Nobody owns Jinx. And Jinx just stole Zuggi's favorite headphones.

A dead-end alley

Jinx didn't run away. Jinx led him here. On purpose.

A glitch-beast: a monster made of broken code, crawling out of the Grid and into the street. And Zuggi, as usual, looks for the quit button.

Kai Vega. Local skater, zero brakes, and terrible at plans. He saw the glitch from three rooftops away and came anyway.

The beast knocks them both flat. Then someone drops in from above.

Rei Kurogane. Nobody knows where she trains or why she carries a katana. She just knows how to cut code.

And she was in the arcade tonight. She saw Zuggi quit.

From Rei's coat comes Byte, a pocket robot built by someone Rei hasn't seen in a year. Byte has been searching the city for one specific player.

Zuggi. Player 1.

The street gives way, and the four of them fall out of Nexa City and into the Grid itself.

High above, in a tower of static, the crowned figure from the Grid Cup final watches them fall. He has a name: the Glitch King.

Next — Chapter 2: Tutorial Level. Inside the Grid, every one of them gets a power. And none of them know how to use it.
