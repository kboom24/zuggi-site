ZUGGI

Zuggi is an entertainment universe built around one cast of characters, the Zuggi Squad. Instead of a games site, a comic site and a video channel that never talk to each other, every story lives in all three.

It's made for everyone: gamers, readers, watchers, kids and grown-ups. Bold, bright and a little bit mischievous, just like its mascot.

Play

Heavy, good-looking browser games (HTML5, WebGL and Unity WebGL) that load instantly. No installs, no downloads.

Read

A vertical-scroll webcomic built for phones first, with new chapters that expand the story.

Watch

An animated series that follows the squad through Neon City, one episode at a time.
